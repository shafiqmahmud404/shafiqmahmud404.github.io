About

I apply GIS, remote sensing and machine learning to hazard susceptibility and risk assessment across Bangladesh. I have co-authored two preprints and a conference paper, and I am currently working on my thesis on machine learning-based agricultural drought risk assessment. I am seeking graduate study to advance uncertainty-aware, geospatial approaches to disaster-resilient planning.
