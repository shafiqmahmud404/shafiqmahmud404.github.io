About

I am a final-year undergraduate in Urban and Regional Planning at CUET with a strong research orientation and a technical foundation in geospatial analysis, remote sensing and machine learning. I am a co-author of two preprints and a conference paper, with a manuscript under review and an undergraduate thesis in progress on machine learning-based agricultural drought risk assessment. I am seeking graduate research opportunities.
